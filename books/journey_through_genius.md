### Afterword
p.286
> This, therefore, is mathematics:
> she gives life to her own discoveries;
> she awakens the mind and purifies the intellect;
> she brings light to our intrinsic ideas;
> she abolishes oblivion and ignorance which are ours by birth.
> — Proclus *A Commentary on the First Book of Euclid's Elements* (trans. by Glenn R. Morrow).

> Mathematics, rightly viewed, possesses not only truth but supreme beauty—a beauty cold and austere, like that of a sculpture, without appeal to any part of our weaker nature, without the gorgeous trappings of painting or music, yet sublimely pure, and capable of a stern perfection such as only the greatest art can show.
> — Bertrand Russell *The Study of Mathematics*
