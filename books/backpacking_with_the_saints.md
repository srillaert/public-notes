p.80
> He [Søren Kierkegaard] said if Luther found it necessary to leave the monastery in the sixteenth century, "the first and foremost duty" of the church today is to return to it. When you're immersed in the mass culture of the modern age, taking seriously a personal spiritual practive becomes the highest priority. It isn't accidental that Kierkegaard employed the pseudonym "Johannes Climacus" in writing some of his most important works. The nom de plume of this seventh-century desert monk allowed him to write from the perspective of a wilderness ascetic inviting personal risk. To be human, he avowed, is to accept the dread of one's aloneness before God.
> — Belden Lane
