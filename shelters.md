# Shelter in Denmark

### Drenderup Shelterplads
55.4123514081558, 9.36557104047425
Saturday 2026-08-08 till Sunday 2026-08-09

Linus, Emil, Hanne and me stayed at this shelter while Anton was at a football competition in Lunderskov.

- Large shelter with a polygonal roof housing a semicircular sleeping area facing a covered fire basket. More than enough place for the four of us, and suitable for our family of six. 
- A grill sits atop the fire pit, and a few old pans let you heat food there.
- No firewood at the shelter, but saws are available, allowing you to collect firewood from fallen trees in the surrounding forest.
- About 300 meters' walk from the parking lot.
- Plenty of wooden tables and benches.
- The toilet is an outhouse over a pit latrine. The was no toilet paper so one has to bring this.
- The temperature dropped during the night to around 11° which was too cold for the summer sleeping bags.
- The surrounding forest is not particularly interesting. The trail to Ødis Sø does not lead directly to the lake; you first need to follow it towards the village of Ødis before reaching the water.
