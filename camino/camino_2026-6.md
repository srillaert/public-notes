# Camino 2026-6-26 till 2026-7-14

## Caminho Português Coastal, Portugal

### 2026-7-1
Albergue Casa do Sardão
Av. de Paço 769
Carreço

Booking through Hugo Lopes at alberguedosardao@gmail.com.
Price 22€ per person,  tourist tax included

+ The small outdoor swimming pool is a nice meeting place for pilgrims. The boys met the two young Irish women there.
+ Lots of common areas where pilgrims can spend time together so the lack of a communal dinner or breakfast is not noticeable.
+ Beautifully decorated with nostalgic items, including hand-drawn family portraits from the 19th century.
+ Our family had an entire attic bedroom to ourselves.
+ We received an unexpected discount for our youngest child.
- The nearest restaurant and grocery store are about half a kilometer away.

[Review on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/YWW6YdfsMxcQtiPH9) : "Albergue Casa do Sardão was the favorite albergue on our family Camino with children, thanks to its many common areas and small outdoor swimming pool.
